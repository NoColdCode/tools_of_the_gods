# Expanded Tools

Beyond the core primal toolkit, TOG adds ranged, utility, and mobility items. All follow the same **level / tier / trait** progression unless noted.

## Summary

| Item | Type | Max level | Notes |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Fishing Rod of the Gods | `FISHING_ROD` | 100 | XP on catch |
| Crossbow of the Gods | `CROSSBOW` | 100 | XP on shot |
| Trident of the Gods | `TRIDENT` | 100 | Melee + thrown |
| Staff of the Gods | `STAFF` | 100 | Right-click divine bolt |
| Wings of the Gods | `WINGS` | 100 | Chest slot glide |

---

## Fishing Rod of the Gods

- **XP:** per catch
- **Traits:** Angler I/II, Reel I, Scholar, Scavenger, Magnetic, Bountiful, utility passives
- **Angler:** bonus treasure + fishing XP (Nautilus Shell → Heart of the Sea)
- **Reel:** faster bites (Cod)

---

## Crossbow of the Gods

- **XP:** on charged shot
- **Traits:** Ranger, Marksman I/II, Quick Load I, Poison, Freezy, Frenzy, Speedy, utility
- **Marksman:** +bolt damage (Spectral Arrow → Arrow)
- **Quick Load:** faster reload (Firework Rocket)

---

## Trident of the Gods

- **XP:** melee hits
- **Traits:** Poison, Freezy, Sharpy, Titan, Impaler I/II, Returning I, Riptide I, Frenzy, Speedy, utility
- **Impaler:** +damage (Prismarine Crystals → Netherite Ingot)
- **Returning:** faster return (Ender Pearl)
- **Riptide:** bonus in rain/water (Prismarine Shard)

---

## Staff of the Gods

- **Use:** Right-click casts a divine bolt (short cooldown)
- **Traits:** Arcane I/II, Channeling I, Scholar, utility
- **Arcane:** stronger bolt, shorter cooldown (Amethyst Shard → Dragon's Breath)
- **Channeling:** lightning in storms (Lightning Rod)

---

## Wings of the Gods

See [Armor and Defense](Armor-and-Defense) for flight and wings traits.

---

## Temple loot

The **Temple of the Gods** structure can spawn leveled versions of crossbow, fishing rod, trident, and staff (levels vary by chest).
