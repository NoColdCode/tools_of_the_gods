# Autosmelt

Bind item: `minecraft:magma_block`

Allowed tools:

- All TOG tools

## Real stats

| Rank | Slot cost | Effect |
|---|---:|---|
| Autosmelt I | 1 | Auto-smelts break and mob drops when smelting recipe exists |
| Autosmelt II | 1 | Same as I, with Shift + Right-Click toggle |

## What it does

Autosmelt replaces eligible drops with their furnace result. If Silk is active, Autosmelt is suppressed.
