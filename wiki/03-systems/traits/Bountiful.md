# Bountiful

Bind item: `minecraft:emerald`

Allowed tools:

- All TOG tools

## Real stats

| Rank | Slot cost | Numeric stat modifiers |
|---|---:|---|
| Bountiful I | 1 | none |

## What it does

Bountiful I is a mechanic-only trait.

- On block drops: each dropped stack has an `8%` chance to be duplicated once.
- Works as an independent proc per stack.
