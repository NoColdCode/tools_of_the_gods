# Broad Touch

Bind item: `minecraft:slime_ball`

Allowed tools:

- Pickaxe
- Hammer
- Shovel
- Axe
- Ultimate Tool

## Real stats

| Rank | Slot cost | Area/tree behavior |
|---|---:|---|
| Broad Touch I | 2 | Pickaxe/Shovel `3x3`, Hammer `5x5`, Axe tree-fell up to `12` logs |
| Broad Touch II | 4 | Mode-based larger area, Axe up to `64` logs |

## What it does

Broad Touch is an area-control mechanic trait.

Mode behavior:

- Axe: Fell 12 / Fell 64
- Hammer and Ultimate: 3x3 / 5x5 / 7x7 / 9x9
- Pickaxe and Shovel: 1x1 / 3x3 / 5x5
