# Bulwark

Bind item: `minecraft:brick`

Allowed tools:

- All TOG tools

## Real stats

| Rank | Slot cost | Numeric stat modifiers |
|---|---:|---|
| Bulwark I | 1 | none |

## What it does

Bulwark I is a mechanic-only trait.

- Grants Resistance I while the tool is held.
- Effect is continuously refreshed.
