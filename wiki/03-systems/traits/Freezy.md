# Freezy

Bind item: `minecraft:ice`

Allowed tools:

- Axe
- Sword
- Bow
- Ultimate Tool

## Real stats

| Rank | Slot cost | On-hit effect | Flat damage |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Freezy I | 1 | Slowness I for `10s` | `-0.5` |
| Freezy II | 2 | Slowness II for `15s` | `-1.0` |

## What it does

Freezy applies slowing control on melee and bow interactions, with a small flat damage tax.
