# Frenzy

Bind item: `minecraft:blaze_powder`

Allowed tools:

- Pickaxe
- Hammer
- Axe
- Shovel
- Hoe
- Sword
- Ultimate Tool

## Real stats

| Rank | Slot cost | Attack speed | Mining speed | Attack damage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Frenzy I | 1 | `+10%` | `+6%` | `-4%` |
| Frenzy II | 2 | `+18%` | `+12%` | `-8%` |
| Frenzy III | 3 | `+28%` | `+18%` | `-12%` |

## What it does

Frenzy is a pressure trait: it speeds up attacks and work rate, but slightly lowers hit strength.

## Synergies

- Berserker Script: Frenzy + Scholar -> `+4%` attack speed
- Ranger Tempo: Ranger + Frenzy -> `+3%` attack speed
