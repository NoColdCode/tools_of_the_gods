# Magnetic

Bind item: `minecraft:copper_ingot`

Allowed tools:

- All TOG tools

## Real stats

| Rank | Slot cost | Effect |
|---|---:|---|
| Magnetic I | 1 | Pulls nearby item entities toward player (about 5-block radius) |

## What it does

Magnetic is a quality-of-life pickup trait that accelerates item collection around the user.
