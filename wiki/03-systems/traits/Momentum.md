# Momentum

Bind item: `minecraft:sugar`

Allowed tools:

- Pickaxe
- Hammer
- Shovel
- Axe
- Ultimate Tool

## Real stats

| Rank | Slot cost | Max blocks tracked | Max mining speed bonus | Idle reset |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Momentum I | 1 | `50` | `+25%` | resets after `3s` idle |
| Momentum II | 2 | `40` | `+40%` | resets after `3s` idle |

## What it does

Momentum ramps mining speed while continuously breaking blocks. The counter collapses after a short idle gap.
