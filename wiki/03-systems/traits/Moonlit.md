# Moonlit

Bind item: `minecraft:phantom_membrane`

Allowed tools:

- Pickaxe
- Hammer
- Axe
- Shovel
- Ultimate Tool

## Real stats

| Rank | Slot cost | Daytime bonus | Night bonus |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Moonlit I | 1 | `+0%` | `+12%` mining speed |
| Moonlit II | 2 | `+0%` | `+20%` mining speed |

## What it does

Moonlit is a special-mechanic trait. It applies an additional mining speed multiplier only when it is night in the world.

## Synergies

- Mooncraft: Moonlit + Scholar -> `+3%` mining speed
