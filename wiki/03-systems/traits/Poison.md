# Poison

Bind item: `minecraft:fermented_spider_eye`

Allowed tools:

- Axe
- Sword
- Bow
- Ultimate Tool

## Real stats

| Rank | Slot cost | On-hit effect | Attack speed |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Poison I | 1 | Poison I for `5s` | `-20%` |
| Poison II | 2 | Poison I for `10s` | `-30%` |

## What it does

Poison adds reliable damage-over-time pressure at the cost of slower attack cadence.
