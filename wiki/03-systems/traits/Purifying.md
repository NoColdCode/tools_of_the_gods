# Purifying

Bind item: `minecraft:glowstone_dust`

Allowed tools:

- All TOG tools

## Real stats

| Rank | Slot cost | Numeric stat modifiers |
|---|---:|---|
| Purifying I | 1 | none |

## What it does

Purifying I is a mechanic-only trait.

- Every `160` ticks (`8s`) while held, removes one active negative effect.
- Beneficial effects are never removed by this trait.
