# Ranger

Bind item: `minecraft:flint`

Allowed tools:

- Bow
- Ultimate Tool

## Real stats

| Rank | Slot cost | XP gain bonus |
|---|---:|---:|
| Ranger I | 1 | `+5%` |
| Ranger II | 2 | `+10%` |

## What it does

Ranger is a specific-item trait family for ranged loadouts. It is intentionally restricted to Bow and Ultimate Tool progression paths.

## Synergies

- Ranger Tempo: Ranger + Frenzy -> `+3%` attack speed
