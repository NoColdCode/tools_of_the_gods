# Scavenger

Bind item: `minecraft:hopper`

Allowed tools:

- All TOG tools

## Real stats

| Rank | Slot cost | Numeric stat modifiers |
|---|---:|---|
| Scavenger I | 1 | none |

## What it does

Scavenger I is a mechanic-only trait.

- Auto-collects block and mob drops directly into player inventory.
- If inventory is full, remaining items stay as world drops.
