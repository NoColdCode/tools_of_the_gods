# Scholar

Bind item: `minecraft:lapis_lazuli`

Allowed tools:

- All TOG tools (including Bow and Ultimate Tool)

## Real stats

| Rank | Slot cost | XP gain bonus |
|---|---:|---:|
| Scholar I | 1 | `+10%` |
| Scholar II | 2 | `+20%` |

## What it does

Scholar is a pure progression trait. It increases XP gain with no direct combat or mining stat tradeoff.

## Synergies

- Berserker Script: Frenzy + Scholar -> `+4%` attack speed
- Mooncraft: Moonlit + Scholar -> `+3%` mining speed
