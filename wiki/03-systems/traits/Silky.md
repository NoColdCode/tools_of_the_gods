# Silky

Bind item: `minecraft:string`

Allowed tools:

- Pickaxe
- Hammer
- Axe
- Shovel
- Hoe
- Sword
- Ultimate Tool

## Real stats

| Rank | Slot cost | Effect |
|---|---:|---|
| Silky I | 1 | Silk touch is always active |
| Silky II | 1 | Silk touch can be toggled with Shift + Right-Click |

## What it does

Silky forces silk-style drops for valid blocks. It is not available on Bow.
