# Speedy

Bind item: `minecraft:redstone_block`

Allowed tools:

- All TOG tools

| Rank | Slot cost | Attack speed | Mining speed | Attack damage | XP gain |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Speedy I | 1 | `+20%` | `+15%` | `-10%` | `-10%` |
| Speedy II | 2 | `+40%` | `+30%` | `-15%` | `-15%` |

## What it does

Speedy is a pure acceleration line. It makes tools and combat faster but trims damage and XP efficiency.
