# Sustaining

Bind item: `minecraft:bread`

Allowed tools:

- All TOG tools

## Real stats

| Rank | Slot cost | Food restore | Saturation restore | Loot penalty |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|
| Sustaining I | 1 | `+1` every `30s` | `+1` every `20s` | `-20%` |
| Sustaining II | 2 | `+1` every `20s` | `+2` every `15s` | `-35%` |
| Sustaining III | 3 | `+2` every `30s` | `+2` every `10s` | `-50%` |

## What it does

Sustaining is a survival sustain line: it keeps hunger and saturation topped while reducing total loot output.
