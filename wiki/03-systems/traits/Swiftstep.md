# Swiftstep

Bind item: `minecraft:feather`

Allowed tools:

- All TOG tools

## Real stats

| Rank | Slot cost | Numeric stat modifiers |
|---|---:|---|
| Swiftstep I | 1 | none |

## What it does

Swiftstep I is a mechanic-only trait.

- Grants Speed I while the tool is held.
- Effect is continuously refreshed.
