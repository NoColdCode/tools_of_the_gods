# Titan

Bind item: `minecraft:anvil`

Allowed tools:

- Axe
- Sword
- Ultimate Tool

## Real stats

| Rank | Slot cost | Flat damage | Attack speed | Mining speed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Titan I | 1 | `+2.0` | `-15%` | `-10%` |
| Titan II | 2 | `+4.0` | `-30%` | `-20%` |

## What it does

Titan converts speed into heavy hits. It is designed for slow, high-impact melee loadouts.

## Synergies

- Iron Pledge: Titan + Soulbound -> `+0.5` flat damage
