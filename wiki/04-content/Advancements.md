## Criteria highlights

- Most advancements use `minecraft:inventory_changed` with specific mod items.
- `arsenal_unfolds` requires all 7 primal tools.
- `orb_scholar` requires all 7 gem tiers (`yellow` through `universe`).
- `ultimate_ascension` requires `ultimate_tool_of_the_gods` and rewards `1500` XP.

## Frames

- Task: early/mid progression
- Goal: arsenal and gem milestones
- Challenge: ultimate ascension
